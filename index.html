About

BALEX is an Oslo-based industrial designer, trained in Denmark, with a strong focus on the future of digital and physical design. Specializing in blockchain technology, tokenization, and virtual fashion, BALEX explores new possibilities where design, ownership, and technology seamlessly converge.

At the center of this work is CALYPTO, a pioneering brand that merges urban tech-wear, digital fashion, and blockchain innovation to redefine the relationship between physical and virtual identities.

With a multidisciplinary approach spanning industrial design, art, and music, BALEX is committed to shaping the next era of creativity in an increasingly digital world.
